1Haley Math Practice — foundations
Everything inherited from the routine app is unchanged: tokens, the 3px bevel, no border-radius, Jersey 25 for numbers and Nunito Sans for sentences, inventory-slot framing, texture only as thin trim, all motion and sound rules, the iron parent zone. Six things are new and they are the work: the emerald, mastery tiers as tool materials, the problem slab, the keypad, factor pair rows, and the 12×12 fact grid.

The one adapted rule: green still means Haley acted, gold still means coins are moving — and now emerald means a gem is moving. Gold redeems for real Minecoins. Emeralds never do. Two piles, two materials, no shared shape.
1aDesign system — new vocabulary beside the inherited tokens
1 · The emerald
Not a recoloured coin. The coin is a flat disc with a soft edge; the emerald is an octagonal cut gem — hard corners, a bright top-left crown, a mid-tone girdle across the middle, a dark pavilion below. Hue is spring green (#12c46b), well clear of the olive grass green and the yellow XP green.
The asset of record is assets/emerald.png (16×16) with emerald-32.png for anything larger — five-colour palette and 1px outline, same discipline as coin.png. Pixel art scales by whole numbers only: display it at 16, 32, 48 or 64 and never in between — 16 and 48 come from the 16px sprite at 1× and 3×, 32 and 64 from the 32px sprite at 1× and 2×. The CSS-drawn gem the build used as a stopgap is retired.

Promotion toast — the badge swaps material under the same bevel
The promotion moment is the inherited advancement toast, nothing new: slides in from the top, self-dismisses in ~3s, never blocks a tap. CHECK is the box-advance: it moves first box → second box → submit, so there is no × key and no separate Next. Tapping either box of the live row focuses it — a typo in the first number must not cost the whole pair. Per-pair Lightning is the orb alone — no FAST chip on factors. At eight pairs a chip would flicker eight times on one card; a small orb on the solved row accumulates instead, and the count still rolls up to 2l. AUTO CHECK never applies here: a factor's length is unknowable in advance.
6 · The 12×12 fact grid — cell anatomy
Parent area only. 24px cells, 2px gaps, headers 18px — 12 columns fit a 390px screen with 14px of margin to spare. The cell carries the material and nothing else: no number, no border per cell. Tap a cell for its fact, tier, and last-seen date.

Section 4 — motion and sound.

4Motion and sound
Inherited rules, no exceptions: transform and opacity only, steps() for anything pixel-art, nothing over 500ms except the award chain, screen changes instant, nothing animates on a failure-adjacent state, nothing blocks a tap. Under prefers-reduced-motion the fades stay and every transform and the gem arc drop. The demos below loop so they can be watched; in the app each fires once, on the action.

Lightning — the same 380ms, three substitutions
0ms — the green overlay lands in steps(1) instead of fading in steps(2) at 60ms. Earlier, not longer.
0ms — the slab carries a 3px #d9ff8a inset rim and the FAST chip. Both static, which is what makes the mark legible under prefers-reduced-motion once every transform is dropped.
110ms — the particles are XP orbs, not stars. Same pop, same 30px rise, same 80ms stagger, same finish.
Total unchanged at 380ms, next card unaffected. Nothing is added to the sequence; three things are swapped inside it.
A finished factors card gets the same beat, not a longer one. Green wash across every row, stars only if no pair was missed, and the same 700ms before the next card — not 900. An eight-pair card already looks four times the achievement of a two-pair one, because four times as much of the screen turns green; buying extra weight with extra waiting charges her most on the card that already took longest. The beat scales itself.

4bSound
Rules — inherited verbatim
Sine only, 20ms exponential attack, exponential decay over dur. Peak gain ≤ .22. No samples, no square waves, nothing percussive. Haley's actions make sound; the parent area is silent end to end. Nothing negative ever sounds. Nothing runs longer than 1.5s. Auto-quiet applies as in the routine app: ×0.5 from 19:00, ×0.25 after the phone is handed over, no exemptions.
Cue table
note(offsetFromCueStart, freqHz, durSeconds, peakGain) — four cues, and that is the whole vocabulary.
Correct answer
EVERY CARD
One blip, dur .10, gain .10. Pitch climbs a pentatonic run through the round, one step per correct card: C6 1046.50 · D6 1174.66 · E6 1318.51 · G6 1567.98 · A6 1760.00, then holds at A6 for the rest of the round. The index resets at round start and does not reset on a miss — the run only ever goes up, so a round audibly fills. Same mechanic as the checklist's scale, at half the duration because there are forty of them. Lightning does not alter this cue in any way.
Round cleared
PER ROUND
(0, 783.99, .18, .14) G5 → (.16, 1046.50, .30, .16) C6. A short resolving figure — the same two notes as the routine app's "last required item done", so "that's the set" sounds the same in both apps.
Emerald award
PER SESSION
(0, 523.25, .25, .16) C5 · (.12, 659.25, .25, .16) E5 · (.24, 783.99, .45, .18) G5 · (.36, 1046.50, .50, .18) C6. Identical shape to the coin award, resolving on the octave, landing with the last gem in the counter. The two currencies sound like siblings on purpose — the same family of reward, different material.
Tier promotion
MILESTONE ONLY
(0, 659.25, .20, .14) E5 → (.18, 830.61, .22, .14) G♯5. A rising major third — distinct from the streak milestone's fifth. Fires for Iron, Gold and Diamond only. If several facts promote in one session the cue plays once, not per fact.
Silent, always
A wrong answer · a slow answer · a duplicate or invalid factor pair · a broken streak · a card coming back around · every keypad press, including CLEAR and CHECK · every screen change · the speed-run timer, running or expired · every round-cleared screen after the cue itself · a Lightning answer · the entire parent area, PIN to lock.
Silence here is the design, not an omission. The app has exactly four sounds; three of them are rare, and the fourth is 100ms long.
Lightning is silent, and here silence is load-bearing rather than restful. The correct blip is a climbing run whose whole job is to make a round sound like it is finishing; a second sound on fast answers would break the run. Worse, once fast answers have their own sound, an ordinary correct answer becomes audibly lesser — a "you were slow" cue by omission. The eye gets the flourish; the ear stays neutral.
What the round sounds like
Five green steps, then a held top note, with the misses as gaps — the shape a round makes in the ear. The gold bar is the round-cleared figure. Nothing occupies the space where a miss happened, which is exactly what makes finishing sound like finishing.
